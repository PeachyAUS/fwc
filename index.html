The Outlier

one number that deserves its own box · new every Monday

52
Fifty two points off the champion in one Sunday afternoon of Mario Golf, and the crown stayed on his head anyway.
Shawn went into Sunday on twelve seventy six and four games later he is on twelve twenty four. He lost the first three to Ferret and the model had him as the favourite in every one of them, which is why they cost him what they did, and he won the fourth. He is second on the ladder now behind Aaron on twelve forty four, and Aaron has not had a game since the twenty second of August. The crown stayed with Shawn all the same, because four weeks idle takes the crown off you until you play again, and Aaron crossed four weeks on Saturday. The rule has done this once before, in August, when Dean B had the best rating in the family, had not played since the twelfth of July, and had to watch somebody else wear it until he came back.
