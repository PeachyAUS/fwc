Making the matches fair

Some of our match ups are not fair fights, and everybody knows it. This is a look at whether we can fix that, using our own 263 games as the evidence. There is a vote at the end. It should take about six minutes to read.

Three lopsided contests. When a game is that one sided, the loser stops wanting to play it and the winner gets nothing out of winning. That is what we are trying to solve.

2. The idea we were asked to test

The suggestion was age. We have a 12 year old and we have a 67 year old, so the thinking was that the tracker should even that out. Beat someone much older and you get a bonus. Lose to someone much older and it costs you less.

Our middle age is 37. Six of us are younger than that, four are older, and three of us are exactly 37. So we tested whether being a long way from 37, in either direction, actually costs you games.

In running around sports, it does

Win rate in physical sports (basketball, table tennis, air hockey, arcade games), by age

That is the shape people expected. It climbs to a peak around 37 to 40 and falls away at both ends. Oscar at the young end and David at the old end are both well below everyone else. So far the age idea looks right.

In the standing still sports, it flips completely

Win rate in precision sports (darts, pool, snooker, golf, putt putt, shuffleboard), by age

Dean B at 58 and Simon at 66 are the two best precision players in the family. If we handed out points for being old, we would be paying them for a disadvantage they do not have, in the sports they are best at.

3. Then the number that settles it

Head to head, every sport, all time
Oscar 11 – 7 Shawn

The 12 year old is beating the 40 year old across the board, not just at Mario Kart.

An age system would have looked at Oscar and Shawn, seen a 28 year gap, and handed Oscar the help. Oscar does not need the help. He is winning.

It goes further. Oscar's Mario Kart rating is 1063. Shawn's is 845. Oscar is the best Mario Kart player in the family and Shawn is the worst, by a long way. Age would have got that exactly backwards.

And the same kid, on the same console, has a Mario Golf record of 2 wins from 11. His age did not change between games. What changed was the sport.

Age tells you what somebody might be able to do. It does not tell you what they actually do. Our own results say the guess is wrong about as often as it is right.

4. So what would actually work

The tracker already keeps a separate rating for every athlete in every sport. Not a guess, a record of how you have really gone. Shawn's Table Tennis rating is 1119. Ferret's is 901. That gap is why the record is 6 nil.

The fix is the one golf has used for a hundred years. A head start, sized by the gap.

How it would work

Every 80 points of gap in a sport is worth roughly a one point head start in that sport. Nothing else changes. You still play the game, you just do not start level when you were never level to begin with.

Match upRating gapHead start
Shawn v Ferret · Table Tennis218Ferret starts 3 up
Oscar v Shawn · Mario Kart218Shawn starts 3 up
Dean B v Cody · Pool92Cody starts 1 up
Aaron v Ferret · Darts39no start, close enough

Notice the second row. The handicap makes the 40 year old the one who gets help against the 12 year old, because in that sport he is the one getting beaten. Age could never have worked that out.

The one rule that has to come with it

Golf keeps two scores, and we would too.

  • The head start decides the night. Who wins the game in the room, who gets the bragging rights.
  • The raw result still feeds your rating. Who actually put the ball in the hole.

Without that split, everyone drifts towards winning half their games, all the ratings squash together, and the ladder stops meaning anything. With it, the games get close and the ladder stays honest.

5. Case studies

Case 1 · Ferret and the table tennis wall

Six matches against Shawn, six losses. Ferret has played 124 games all up, more than anyone except Shawn, and his table tennis rating of 901 is the lowest of the ten of us who play it.

With a handicap: Ferret starts 3 points up. On his six losses so far, that gap would have made at least a couple of them live contests into the back end. He does not get given a win. He gets given a chance.

Fixes it Age would also have flagged this one, but backwards. Ferret is 30 and Shawn is 40, so an age system would have given the head start to the wrong man.

Case 2 · Shawn and the Mario Kart problem

Seven races against Oscar, seven losses. Shawn's Mario Kart rating is 845, which is the lowest rating any of us holds in any sport we play regularly.

With a handicap: Shawn starts 3 places up, or Oscar gives him a lap start, whatever suits the game. Suddenly there is a race on.

Age gets this wrong An age system would have handed the 12 year old a bonus for beating a 40 year old, in the one sport where the 12 year old is the best in the family.

Case 3 · David, and why one number hides two stories

David's overall win rate is 22%, the second lowest in the competition, and it would be easy to put that down to being 67. But split it by sport and it is not one story at all.

Case 4 · Dean B, the one an age system would punish

58 years old. Third on the ladder. The best win rate in the family at 62%. Unbeaten in four at the car racing arcade, 70% at shuffleboard, 71% at table tennis.

With a handicap: he gives head starts, because he has earned that by winning. That is the system working.

With an age system: he collects a bonus for being 58, on top of a record that says he does not need one.

Age gets this wrong

6. What it would cost us to run

Being straight about the limits. A handicap is only as good as the games behind it, and some of our sports have barely been played.

Ready now, enough gamesNot yet, too thin
Table Tennis · ShuffleBoard · Mario Golf · FIFA · Darts · Mario Kart · Mario Party HORSE · Chess · Poker · Putt Putt · Golf · Air Hockey · Pool · Cards · Call of Duty · the arcade games

Seven of our twenty played sports could carry a handicap today. The rest need more games first. Air Hockey has been played six times total, so there is nothing there to work from yet.

And in most sports the gaps are small enough that the handicap would be nothing at all. Darts runs from Aaron at 1046 down to Shawn at 982, a gap of 64 across nine players, so almost nobody would get a start. Only Table Tennis and Mario Kart are wide enough right now to really change a game.

This is why the proposal is for next season, starting January. By then we will have another few hundred games in the book and most of the thin sports fill in on their own.

7. What we recommend

Skip the age curve. Bring in sport handicaps in January.

The age idea was worth testing and it was right about one thing: some of our match ups are not fair. But when we checked it against 263 real games it got Oscar wrong, it got Shawn wrong, it got Dean B wrong and it got David half wrong. It is a guess about ability, and we already have the real thing sitting in the tracker.

Handicaps use what actually happened. They point the help at whoever is losing, in the sport they are losing at, no matter how old they are. They shrink by themselves as you improve, and disappear when you no longer need them. And nobody has to talk about anybody's birthday.
